# Data dump
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/Stargate/comments/z8ww6s/a_sg1_viewing_order_with_only_crossover_episodes/


I figured I'd share this with all of you for anyone interested in a SG-1 rewatch with only the crossover/tie-in episodes from spinoffs Atlantis (including every Season 4 episode Sam appears in as a regular) and Universe (even including Daniel's full tutorial from the DVDs and Blu-Rays), also for any first-time viewers only interested in the SG-1 ensamble's story. I did something similar for Arrow where the crossovers were much heavier there and on its spinoffs, in recently going through the Stargate franchise I had the urge to come up with something similar for this incredible franchise.

This viewing order will only include SG-1's last three seasons and again just crossover episodes from the two spinoffs, in addition to the two follow-up SG-1 features where chronologically appropriate. Enjoy!
